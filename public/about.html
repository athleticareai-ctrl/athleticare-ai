What it won't do

AthletiCare doesn't diagnose injuries and isn't a replacement for an athletic trainer or doctor. Its job is to help you notice when something is off and get you to the right person sooner.

Skip the app and see someone right away if you have:

  • A hit to the head, or a headache, dizziness, or confusion after one
  • A joint that looks out of place or can't hold weight
  • Numbness, tingling, or pain that keeps getting worse
